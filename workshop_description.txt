While working on another mod I ran into this vanilla bug and was able to fix it:

Since B42's giant trees, a crown next to a building can cover your rooms from the outside. Standing indoors behind it, your interior is hard to see. On upper floors it can vanish behind the foliage completely.

Clear Canopy hides a giant tree while you are indoors and its crown covers your character. Step outside and it's back. No options, no keybinds.

Once The Indie Stone fixes this officially, just unsubscribe. Nothing is left in your save.
